## 6.1 Contributions

§3.10.3 states the gap this study closes: the field has settled on a standard pipeline for micro-expression recognition but has never measured which of its stages are responsible for the result, leaving a practitioner no basis for allocating effort. §4.1.1 puts the operative question accordingly — not how well the pipeline performs, but which of its stages are responsible for that performance, and by how much. The thesis answers it. Four contributions follow, in descending order of what each is worth.

- **A matched-pair factorial measurement of the standard pipeline.** The reviewed corpus evaluates components only inside complete systems, so an improvement can seldom be attributed to the part credited with it (§3.10.2). One reviewed study does toggle a single stage against an otherwise fixed pipeline — Li et al.'s magnification sweep, carrying α = 1 as an explicit no-magnification arm (§3.2.3) — but that stage is a preprocessing step. No reviewed study isolates a *learned* component, whether a convolutional backbone, an attention module or a temporal encoder, against an otherwise identical pipeline, and none varies several jointly so that their interactions can be read. Twelve configurations cover every architecturally valid cell of a 2⁴ matrix, each trained from scratch and scored under complete 25-fold leave-one-subject-out with every non-varied factor held identical (§4.1.1, §4.3).

- **The proposed unified system is not this study's best configuration.** `config_2_temporal_only`, a temporal transformer applied to the motion tensor alone, reaches **0.7122** pooled macro F1 against `config_8_proposed_unified`'s **0.6659** (§5.1.2); and the component consuming most of the sweep's compute is the only one whose mean effect is negative (§5.5.4, §5.8.1). This is the thesis's most useful finding, and the design produced it rather than conceding it. An end-to-end study reporting the proposed system alone would have published the lower of those two numbers, with no way to discover that a subset of its own architecture outperformed it.

- **The shape of the effect distribution across the four components.** The contribution is not any individual mean but the form of the distribution: of the effect this design measures, almost all concentrates in one component, a second is harmful on average, and the remaining two cannot be distinguished from noise at this sample size (§5.8.1). §5.8.2 makes the structural point — an end-to-end evaluation returns a single number and so cannot exhibit a distribution at all.

- **A documented account of what was excluded.** §4.6.7 records four earlier evaluations of the same matrix and the property of each that disqualifies it: a data-routing defect, degenerate class support, incomplete fold or configuration coverage. Reporting one complete protocol rather than the most favourable of five is itself a methodological position, and one the reviewed corpus rarely documents (§3.10.2).
