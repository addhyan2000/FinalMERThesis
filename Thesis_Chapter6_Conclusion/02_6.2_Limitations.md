## 6.2 Limitations

§5.8.3 gathers the limitations bearing on the results themselves, and its closing bullet — that each component is varied only as present or absent — is the hinge into this section. What follows is what no single chapter gathers: the limits of the study as a whole, and what the fixed settings behind that bullet cost.

**The ablation measures architecture at one point in preprocessing space.** §3.10.4 records that α = 10, T = 32, λ = 10⁻⁴ and γ = 2.0 are inherited from the literature and held fixed throughout, never swept, even though §3.10.2 establishes that such parameters have interior optima locatable only by exhaustive search. Every effect in Chapter 5 is conditional on those values, and this design cannot say how any would move if they changed.

**Ten declared divergences from the methods reviewed.** §3.10.5 ledgers them; three bear directly on how the results should be read. The convolutional stem carries no temporal kernels, so what was measured is a learned *spatial* stem rather than spatio-temporal convolution (§3.6.6). It runs at 224 × 224 against a literature norm at or below 100 × 100, so its negative effect may be a resolution result as much as an architectural one — a reading §5.5.5 advances and then qualifies, since the same source reports shallow models to be largely robust to resolution. And magnification is applied after subsampling, so its realised pass-band is clip-dependent and the component was measured outside its intended specification (§3.5.7). None invalidates the ablation — every configuration shares them — but each narrows what the corresponding result is a result *about*.

**One corpus, one demographic, trained from scratch, with no composite training.** §3.1.5 states the cost of that choice directly: no claim in this thesis extends to other populations, other capture conditions, or other corpora. §3.10.4 is explicit on this point: what these components do on a single small corpus without pre-training is a separate question from how they behave under composite-database training, not an answer to it, and no cross-dataset claim is made anywhere.

These three bound what the study is valid for. The measurements report what four components contribute to this pipeline, on this corpus, at one fixed setting of the preprocessing parameters, under the divergences §3.10.5 records. Within that scope they are matched-pair measurements against a single complete protocol; outside it they are not evidence, and nothing in Chapter 5 is offered as though they were.
